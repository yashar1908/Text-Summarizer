# 🏥 Medical AI Chatbot  

This project is an **AI-powered chatbot** designed to intelligently **summarize medical queries** into a structured, easy-to-read format. It helps users communicate symptoms, medical history, and concerns in a way that is **clear, concise, and clinically relevant**.  

## **🌟 What This Project Achieves**  

🔹 **Transforms complex user queries into structured medical summaries**  
🔹 **Extracts key health concerns, symptoms, history, and questions**  
🔹 **Provides properly formatted responses using Markdown**  
🔹 **Enhances communication between patients and healthcare professionals**  
🔹 **Ensures critical medical details are preserved while removing unnecessary information**  

### **📝 How It Works**  
1. The user submits a free-text medical query.  
2. The AI extracts key details and organizes them into:  
   - **Issues/Concerns**  
   - **Symptoms**  
   - **Relevant Medical History**  
   - **Questions/Requests**  
3. The structured summary is returned in a **clear, readable format**.  

### **🔍 Example Transformation**  

#### **User Input:**  
*"I’ve been having headaches and dizziness for two weeks. I also have a history of migraines and take blood pressure medication. Should I see a doctor?"*  

#### **AI-Generated Summary:**  
**1. Issues/Concerns:**  
- Persistent headaches and dizziness for two weeks.  
- Concerns about medication side effects.  

**2. Symptoms:**  
- Headaches.  
- Dizziness.  

**3. Relevant Medical History:**  
- History of migraines.  
- On blood pressure medication.  

**4. Questions/Requests:**  
- Could this be a medication side effect?  
- Should they seek medical consultation?  

### **🌍 Why This Matters**  
In medical settings, **clear communication is crucial**. Patients often provide **unstructured, lengthy descriptions**, which can make it difficult for healthcare professionals to **quickly assess the issue**. This AI chatbot **bridges that gap** by converting unstructured input into a **precise, clinically relevant summary**.
